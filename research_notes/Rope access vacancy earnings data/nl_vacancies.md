## 3. Netherlands income tax facts (2026), single filer, no dependents

All figures below were freshly verified via web search on 2026-09-27 against secondary sources aggregating Belastingdienst (Dutch Tax Administration) figures; primary belastingdienst.nl pages appeared in search results but were not independently fetched (per task guidance not to over-invest if the primary site is hard to reach). Treat the primary belastingdienst.nl URLs listed as the authoritative cross-check.

### Loonheffing (combined wage tax + national insurance) brackets, Box 1, 2026
- **Bracket 1: 35.75%** on income up to **€38,883**.
- **Middle bracket: 37.56%** on income from €38,883 to €78,426 — but this middle rate applies **only to people at/above state-pension (AOW) age** (specifically those who no longer pay the AOW premium component, e.g. born before 1958 per one source's framing). Below state pension age, the 35.75% bracket effectively runs up to €78,426 in practice because the AOW premium (part of the 35.75%/37.56% figure) continues to apply, then jumps straight to the top rate.
- **Top rate: 49.50%** on income above **€78,426**.
- Note: these are the confirmed figures the user already had (35.75% / 37.56% / 49.50%) — verified current for 2026, with the nuance above about who actually pays the 37.56% middle rate. Source: [Dutch Payroll Tax 2026 guide](https://dutchtaxcalculators.com/dutch-payroll-tax-loonheffing-2026-complete-guide-for-employees-employers/), [iamexpat.nl Dutch tax system](https://www.iamexpat.nl/expat-info/money-taxation/dutch-tax-system), cross-referenced against [Belastingdienst rates page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontenten/belastingdienst/business/payroll_taxes/you_are_not_established_in_the_netherlands_are_you_required_to_withhold_payroll_taxes/when_you_are_going_to_withhold_payroll_taxes/calculating_payroll_taxes/rates/rates) (not independently fetched).
- Effective-rate illustration from the same source: because 27.65 of the 35.75 percentage points in bracket 1 is actually social-security premium (not "tax" per se) and is offset for most earners by tax credits, a €40,000 earner nets an effective rate of roughly 15.5%, a €60,000 earner roughly 25%, and an €80,000 earner roughly 29.8%, after applying the combined heffingskortingen (up to ~€8,800 combined credits).

### Arbeidskorting (employment tax credit), 2026
- Maximum credit: **€5,685** (up from €5,599 in 2025); for AOW-age recipients, max is €2,840.
- Builds up in three phases as income rises:
  - Phase 1 (€0–€11,965): 8.324% of income, reaching €996 at the top of the phase.
  - Phase 2 (€11,965–€25,845): €996 + 31.009% of income above €11,965, reaching €5,300.
  - Phase 3 (€25,845–€45,592): €5,300 + 1.950% of income above €25,845, reaching the €5,685 maximum.
- Phase-out: above **€45,592** income, the credit is reduced by **6.51%** of income above that threshold.
- Source: [Belastingdienst arbeidskorting table 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/prive/inkomstenbelasting/heffingskortingen_boxen_tarieven/heffingskortingen/arbeidskorting/tabel-arbeidskorting-2026) (link found, not independently fetched), corroborated by [Salure](https://salure.nl/nieuws/arbeidskorting) and [rekenmachinepro.nl](https://rekenmachinepro.nl/blog/arbeidskorting-2026-tabel-bedragen/).

### Mandatory basic health insurance (zorgverzekering) premium and zorgtoeslag, 2026
- Average mandatory basic health insurance premium for 2026: **≈€1,884/year (≈€157/month)** — this is an average across insurers; actual premium varies by provider and chosen policy/deductible.
- Zorgtoeslag (healthcare allowance) income threshold for **single people, 2026: €40,857/year**. Above this income, no zorgtoeslag is payable. (Combined household threshold with a tax partner: €51,142/year.)
- Maximum zorgtoeslag for single people, 2026: **€129/month**.
- Asset (vermogen) limit for zorgtoeslag eligibility, 2026: €146,011 for a single person (€184,633 combined with a partner).
- Zorgtoeslag amount is independent of which insurer/premium you actually pay — it is calculated purely from income (and assets), not your actual premium.
- Source: [poliswijzer.nl zorgtoeslag 2026](https://www.poliswijzer.nl/zorgverzekering/kennisbank/zorgtoeslag), [Belastingdienst/Dienst Toeslagen conditions page](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/nl/zorgtoeslag/content/kan-ik-zorgtoeslag-krijgen) (link found, not independently fetched), [rekenmachinepro.nl zorgtoeslag berekenen 2026](https://rekenmachinepro.nl/blog/zorgtoeslag-berekenen-2026/).

### ZZP / self-employed vs. regular employment — how the calculation differs
Relevant because many offshore rope-access/wind technicians in NL work as zzp (self-employed) contractors rather than as CAO-covered employees:

- **Zelfstandigenaftrek (self-employed deduction), 2026: a fixed €2,470** deducted from profit, available if the "hours criterion" is met (minimum **1,225 hours/year** worked in the business). Note this deduction has been shrinking year over year under government policy (was much higher in prior years) and continues to be phased down.
- **MKB-winstvrijstelling (SME profit exemption), 2026: 12.7%** of profit, applied *after* the zelfstandigenaftrek is subtracted (so it's calculated on the lower, post-deduction profit figure — the two reliefs stack without double-counting by design). No hours-criterion requirement for this one.
- Combined effect: a zzp'er's taxable profit = (gross profit − business costs − zelfstandigenaftrek €2,470) × (1 − 12.7%), and *that* net figure is what gets taxed at the Box 1 income-tax rates above — meaningfully lower effective tax than an employee earning the same gross amount.
- **No employer-side withholding for zzp'ers:** unlike an employee, a zzp contractor receives gross invoiced amounts with no loonheffing withheld at source — the contractor is personally responsible for setting aside and paying their own income tax and the income-dependent Zvw (Zorgverzekeringswet) healthcare-law contribution themselves, typically via quarterly provisional assessments or year-end settlement, rather than an employer withholding tax and paying it monthly.
- Zzp'ers also do not have an employer paying the employer's share of sector/UWV social-insurance premiums (WW, WIA, etc.) that a regular employee's payroll cost includes — meaning a zzp day-rate that looks like it equals an employee's gross day-rate is not actually equivalent take-home, since the zzp'er must separately fund retirement savings, sick-pay/disability insurance (arbeidsongeschiktheidsverzekering), and holiday-pay-equivalent buffers that a CAO employee gets automatically.
- Sources: [Informer.nl MKB-winstvrijstelling 2026](https://www.informer.nl/belastingen/aftrekposten/mkb-winstvrijstelling), [deonlinerekenmachine.nl zelfstandigenaftrek 2026](https://deonlinerekenmachine.nl/zelfstandigenaftrek-en-mkb-winstvrijstelling-2026-bedragen-en-berekening/), [Belastingdienst mkb-winstvrijstelling 2026](https://www.belastingdienst.nl/wps/wcm/connect/bldcontentnl/belastingdienst/zakelijk/winst/inkomstenbelasting/veranderingen-inkomstenbelasting-2026/mkb-winstvrijstelling-2026) (link found, not independently fetched).
