# Dutch Engineering, Energy & Offshore Job Market — High-Paying Roles (2026)

## Which job titles pay the highest annual gross salary, by seniority?

### Takeaway
Chemical/process engineering (oil, gas, chemicals, pharma) and high-voltage/grid engineering command the top salaries among "regular" engineering roles (€60k–€103k realistic total range for mid/senior with real 2026 postings confirming both ends). Maritime/offshore construction engineering at large Dutch marine contractors (Van Oord, Boskalis) and senior/lead engineer titles show the widest upside (reported up to ~€160k–€200k at senior/principal level, though this figure looks unusually high and should be treated cautiously). Offshore wind turbine technicians and rope-access/NDT inspectors earn less in base salary than the engineers, but offshore/rotational allowances, overtime and shift premiums lift effective take-home meaningfully, and offshore/technical-elastomer/day-rate contract work adds a large premium over onshore equivalents.

### Cited Findings
- Real live posting: Chemours (Dordrecht) "Process Engineer – Fluoro Elastomers" (MSc Chemical Engineering, 5+ yrs experience) lists an explicit pay range of **€62,080–€97,000/year** ("Pay Range in local currency"), plus 13th month, travel allowance, 33 vacation days — posted May 18, 2026. — [Indeed job listing](https://to.indeed.com/aasqhyqrp7l9)
- Real live posting: Hanab BV (Zwolle) "Lead Engineer High Voltage (Primary Systems)" — bachelor's in Mechanical/Power Engineering — explicit **annual salary €63,425–€103,133** (gross monthly €4,343–€7,062, + 10% flex budget, 3.7% year-end bonus, 8% holiday allowance, 27% ABP pension contribution, CAO Netwerkbedrijven collective agreement) — posted Sept 14, 2026. — [Indeed job listing](https://to.indeed.com/aaxjbqkz2klm)
- National average process engineer gross salary in the Netherlands: **€68,135**/year (avg bonus €2,180); entry level (1–3 yrs) **€48,216**; senior (8+ yrs) **€75,965**; Amsterdam average **€79,918** (range €55,623–€97,260); Rotterdam average **€75,909**. — [ERI SalaryExpert / Glassdoor via search](https://www.salaryexpert.com/salary/job/process-engineer/netherlands)
- Energy Engineer average pay in Netherlands: **€58,603**/year (range €40,319–€71,554); energy/utilities majors (Shell, TotalEnergies) pay average **€48,000–€55,000**/year for engineers, with experience-based raises up to +€15,000/year. — [search result summary, thedutchdaily.nl salary guide](https://thedutchdaily.nl/netherlands-salary-guide-2026-average-wages-it-engineering-marketing/)
- General "Project Engineer" (all sectors) Netherlands average: **€81,967**/year; entry level (1–3 yrs) **€57,087**; senior (8+ yrs) **€101,840**. — [PayScale/ERI via search](https://www.payscale.com/research/NL/Job=Project_Engineer/Salary)
- "Project Engineer Offshore" specific-title average is notably lower than the general Project Engineer figure: **€44,738–€45,004**/year (25th–75th percentile €43,374–€46,634) — [Glassdoor via search](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/netherlands-project-engineer-offshore-salary-SRCH_IL.0,11_IN178_KO12,37.htm)
- At Boskalis, offshore project engineers reportedly earn **€57K–€62K**/year (median €60K); at Van Oord, offshore construction managers earn **€65K–€69K** (median €67K). A separate, much higher figure for "Engineer at Van Oord" broadly (avg **€124,433**/year, 25th–75th percentile €97,069–€161,267, up to ~€202,260 at 90th percentile) appeared in search results but looks inflated/likely mixing in senior management or non-representative self-reported data — treat with caution and prefer the offshore-specific figures. — [Glassdoor via search](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salary/Van-Oord-Engineer-Netherlands-Salaries-EJI_IE142320.0,8_KO9,17_IL.18,29_IN178.htm)
- Offshore wind turbine technician: competitive Netherlands salaries **€45,000–€75,000**/year with offshore allowances; total employment cost cited as high as €45,000–€120,000 depending on experience/onshore vs offshore. — [iPS Powerful People via search](https://ipspowerfulpeople.com/knowledgebase/how-much-do-offshore-wind-technicians-make/)
- Real live posting: Tasman Professionals (recruiter) "windturbine monteur offshore" (Noord-Holland/North Sea nearshore wind parks) — explicit gross monthly salary "**v.a. €3,250**" (from €3,250, i.e. ~€45,500+/yr before bonuses, "maatwerk"/negotiable), 8% holiday allowance, 27 vacation days + 13 ADV days, paid overtime, shift/offshore allowances, direct contract (year contract or permanent, no agency secondment) — posted March 3, 2026, MBO/HBO technical diploma (Elektrotechniek/Mechatronica/AOT), no prior experience strictly required (training provided). — [Indeed job listing](https://to.indeed.com/aacqtwczf6mm)
- Real live posting: Vattenfall (Slootdorp, Prinses Ariane Windpark) "Service Technician – Onshore Wind Turbines" — MBO-3/4 electrotechnical diploma required, no salary figure disclosed ("marktconform salaris" / market-conforming salary only) — posted Dec 16, 2025. — [Indeed job listing](https://to.indeed.com/aaymxp2rxcyv)
- Rope access technician Netherlands: figures conflict across sources — one source cites Netherlands average **€56,095**/year (25th–75th percentile €53,659–€58,532); another cites average gross **€43,191**/year; entry-level (1–3 yrs) **€31,223**, senior (8+ yrs) **€48,342**. Sources do not separate the IRATA-certification premium specifically. — [Glassdoor / ERI SalaryExpert via search](https://www.salaryexpert.com/salary/job/rope-access-technician/netherlands); contradicted on exact figure by [Rope Access Network blog](https://www.ropeaccess.network/blog/rope-access-salary-guide)
- Offshore NDT inspector (general, not Netherlands-specific): day rates cited around **$560–$800/day mid-career** and **$820–$1,200/day senior**, annualized **$107,500–$152,500** (mid) and **$155,000–$227,500** (senior) in USD; North Sea contractor day rates (broader region) **£320–£820/day** depending on seniority, with Danish/Dutch sectors "between UK and Norway." Onshore Netherlands NDT roles pay much less: **€3,400–€5,500 gross/month** (~€40,800–€66,000/yr). Offshore/sour-service assignments pay 30–50% above onshore; advanced techniques (phased array, TOFD) pay 25–40% above conventional NDT. — [Rigzone / Atlantis NDT salary guide via search](https://atlantisndt.com/blog/ndt-salary-guide-2026-global)

### Inferences
- The two live, verified engineer postings (Chemours process engineer, Hanab lead HV engineer) both cluster in the same real-world band of roughly **€62k–€103k/year** for mid-to-senior technical engineering roles at Dutch industrial/grid employers in 2026 — this is a more trustworthy anchor than the wider, more volatile aggregator averages.
- "Offshore" as a qualifier on a job title does not automatically mean higher pay for salaried engineer roles (the Glassdoor "Project Engineer Offshore" figure is actually lower than generic "Project Engineer") — the real premium for offshore work shows up in technician/inspector/contractor day-rate and allowance structures, not necessarily base salary for engineers.
- For someone newly established (i.e., without deep NL-specific network or years of local experience), technician-track entry via MBO/HBO diploma + employer-paid GWO/certification training (as in the Vattenfall and Tasman listings) appears to be a more accessible high-paying entry point than direct-hire process/lead engineer roles which required MSc + 5+ years or HBO + strong technical specialization.

### Gaps
- No official/primary-source Dutch government (CBS) wage statistics were retrieved for these specific job titles; all figures come from aggregator/estimator sites (Glassdoor, PayScale, ERI SalaryExpert) or the ~5 live job postings collected, several of which don't disclose salary.
- Could not access Loonwijzer.nl or a Brunel/Hays NL sector-specific salary guide PDF directly within budget; only secondary summaries of "Brunel salary" via SEO aggregator pages were found, and those returned recruiter-staff salaries (Brunel's own employees) rather than the engineering placements Brunel makes — this is a notable gap since Brunel is one of the largest Dutch technical/energy staffing agencies.
- "Energy transition project manager" and "maritime/offshore engineer" as distinct standalone job-title salary bands could not be pinned down with dedicated aggregator pages; only related titles (Energy Engineer, Project Engineer, offshore construction manager) were found.

## What is current demand/vacancy volume like for each role?

### Takeaway
Offshore wind is a confirmed, structural shortage sector in the Netherlands and Europe generally — GWO/high-voltage-certified offshore technicians are explicitly named as scarce, inflating labor costs 15–20% year-on-year. Vacancy counts on Glassdoor/LinkedIn aggregators show hundreds of open roles at any time for "offshore wind," "project engineer," and "wind energy" search terms in the Netherlands, consistent with high demand across engineering/technician tiers.

### Cited Findings
- "The Netherlands is experiencing a shortage of certified offshore technicians, especially those with high-voltage and GWO (Global Wind Organisation) certification, which is constraining service capacity and inflating labor costs by 15–20% year-on-year." — [search result summary, v-hr.com / iPS Powerful People](https://www.v-hr.com/blog/offshore-wind-hiring-crisis-skills-shortage/)
- At the European level, cited gaps: **7,000 blade technicians, 6,500 field engineers, and 5,000 pre-assembly technicians** shortage across the continent; described as "structural, not temporary," because industry growth outpaced training pipelines. — [search summary, crewbase.pro maritime/offshore blog](https://crewbase.pro/blog/2026/02/offshore-wind-careers-2026)
- Roles named as currently in highest offshore demand: offshore wind technicians, dynamic positioning officers, subsea engineers, HSE advisors with renewables experience, marine warranty surveyors. — [crewbase.pro via search](https://crewbase.pro/blog/2026/02/offshore-wind-careers-2026)
- Vacancy-volume signals from job aggregators (snapshot, not verified independently): Glassdoor lists **81 "offshore wind" jobs** in Netherlands (Aug 2026 snapshot), **18 "wind turbine technician" jobs** (Sept 2026 snapshot), **373 "offshore project engineer" jobs** (Jul 2026 snapshot), and LinkedIn NL lists **183 "Wind Energy" vacancies** (with "6 nieuw"/6 new noted at time of search). — [Glassdoor Netherlands listings via search](https://www.glassdoor.com/Job/netherlands-offshore-wind-jobs-SRCH_IL.0,11_IN178_KO12,25.htm); [LinkedIn NL Wind Energy jobs](https://nl.linkedin.com/jobs/wind-energy-jobs)
- Major offshore wind employers actively recruiting technicians in NL per search snapshot: Siemens Gamesa (Offshore Service Technicians, Groningen), Vestas (Service Technician Offshore, Den Helder, HKW VII windpark), Deutsche Windtechnik (offshore service technicians, NL). — [search summary of LinkedIn/company listings](https://nl.linkedin.com/jobs/wind-energy-jobs)
- "The Netherlands is about to see a huge ramp up of wind energy in the next few years, particularly offshore" — general industry-growth claim (framed as forward-looking/narrative, treat as directional rather than a hard statistic). — [search summary](https://nl.linkedin.com/jobs/wind-energy-jobs)

### Inferences
- Demand ranking (high to lower) based on shortage language and vacancy volume: **Offshore wind technicians (GWO/HV-certified) = high/shortage**; **Process/manufacturing engineers at industrial majors (Chemours, J&J, Shell, Air Liquide) = high** (multiple concurrent live postings found across different companies in one search pass); **High-voltage/grid engineers (grid operators, Hanab-type EPC contractors) = high**, driven by the Dutch energy-transition grid-expansion buildout (TenneT-linked work explicitly mentioned in the Hanab posting); **Rope access/NDT inspectors = medium**, real Indeed.nl search for this exact combined term returned zero direct results, suggesting either lower current listing volume under that specific title or that such roles are posted under other titles (e.g., "Inspector," "Technical Specialist") more often.

### Gaps
- No official vacancy-count or shortage-occupation-list data was retrieved directly from a Dutch government or industry-association primary source (e.g., RVO, UWV "krapte-indicator"/shortage-occupation list, or NWEA — Nederlandse WindEnergie Associatie). This would be a valuable primary-source addition if accessible.
- A direct Indeed.nl search for "rope access technician NDT inspector" in the Netherlands returned **no results** in this research pass — this may indicate the search term itself is too narrow/combined rather than true absence of demand; a separate search using only "rope access" or only "NDT inspector" was not attempted due to the task's instruction not to repeat searches per role, so vacancy volume specifically for this title in NL remains unconfirmed.

## Entry barriers: certifications, Dutch language, and work-permit/sponsorship requirements

### Takeaway
Barriers vary sharply by role tier: offshore wind technician roles require GWO Basic Safety Training (and refreshers every 2 years) plus a technical MBO/HBO diploma, with Dutch language required for some direct-utility postings (Vattenfall) but not for agency-facilitated openings (Tasman) which accept English only; process/lead engineer roles at multinationals require a relevant BSc/MSc plus years of experience, with English as the operative working language and Dutch treated as "a plus" rather than mandatory. For non-EU nationals, the Highly Skilled Migrant (kennismigrant) visa route requires meeting a fixed minimum gross monthly salary threshold that rises annually — for 2026, the applicable floor is roughly €4,357/month (under 30) or €5,942/month (30 and over), which most of the engineer-tier roles found here clear comfortably but which the technician-tier entry salary (~€3,250/month from the Tasman posting) does NOT clear.

### Cited Findings
- **GWO Basic Safety Training (BST)** is the baseline certification for offshore/onshore wind technician work in the Netherlands: covers five core competencies (Fire Awareness, First Aid, Sea Survival and Transfer, Working at Heights, Manual Handling); minimum age 18 with valid ID; requires a Medical Self-Assessment Form; valid for 24 months, after which a Basic Safety Training Refresher (BSTR) is required. Offshore-specific roles additionally require the Sea Survival module. Training is available in the Netherlands via providers such as DHTC (Den Helder, NL's offshore logistics hub). — [GWO standard summary via search](https://www.globalwindsafety.org/standards/basic-safety-training-standard); [DHTC course listing](https://www.dhtc.nl/en/courses/gwo-wind/)
- Vattenfall's live posting explicitly requires an MBO-3/4 diploma (electrotechnical or comparable technical background: Algemene Operationele Techniek, Scheepswerktuigkunde, Energietechniek, Elektrotechniek, or Werktuigkunde), a category-B driver's license, physical fitness with a mandatory medical test, no fear of heights/claustrophobia, and explicitly requires **both Dutch and English** reading/writing proficiency, plus residence in Noord-Holland or Friesland. — [Indeed listing](https://to.indeed.com/aaymxp2rxcyv)
- Tasman Professionals' (agency) offshore technician posting requires only an MBO/HBO technical diploma and English language ability (no Dutch requirement stated), a driver's license, and medical fitness — and explicitly welcomes career-starters ("Geen ervaring vereist"/no experience required), with the employer/agency providing training. — [Indeed listing](https://to.indeed.com/aacqtwczf6mm)
- Chemours' Process Engineer posting requires an MSc in Chemical Engineering or related discipline, 5+ years of process engineering/manufacturing technology/reliability experience, with at least 3 years specifically as a plant process engineer; language requirement is "**fluent in English; Dutch is a plus (willingness to learn is sufficient)**" — i.e., Dutch fluency is explicitly NOT required. — [Indeed listing](https://to.indeed.com/aasqhyqrp7l9)
- Hanab's Lead Engineer High Voltage posting requires a completed bachelor's degree in Mechanical Engineering, Power Engineering, or comparable technical field; no explicit Dutch-language requirement stated in the posting text (posting itself is written in English, suggesting an English-friendly workplace), operates under the Dutch CAO Netwerkbedrijven (network companies collective labour agreement). — [Indeed listing](https://to.indeed.com/aaxjbqkz2klm)
- **2026 Highly Skilled Migrant (kennismigrant) minimum gross salary thresholds** (IND, effective Jan 1, 2026): **€5,942/month** for migrants aged 30 and over; **€4,357/month** for migrants under 30; a further reduced threshold of **€3,122/month** applies to a specific sub-category (e.g., recent graduates from a Dutch institution, per standard IND rules, though the specific eligibility for this reduced rate was not detailed in the retrieved snippet). Thresholds rose ~4.5% versus 2025 and apply to all new applications, extensions, and change-of-employer notifications with a 2026 start date; salary must be fixed, gross, monthly, market-based, and paid into the migrant's bank account, excluding holiday allowance. — [Baker Tilly NL / Jobbatical / Grant Thornton NL via search](https://www.bakertilly.nl/en/inzichten/kennisartikel/new-threshold-amounts-for-expats-and-highly-skilled-migrants-2026); [Jobbatical summary](https://www.jobbatical.com/blog/netherlands-highly-skilled-migrant-salary-thresholds-2026)

### Inferences
- Comparing the 2026 HSM thresholds against the concrete salaries found: the Chemours process engineer range (€62,080–€97,000/yr ≈ €5,173–€8,083/month before holiday allowance) and the Hanab lead engineer range (€4,343–€7,062/month gross) both clear the ≥30 HSM threshold (€5,942/month) at their mid-to-upper band, but the lower end of each range (Chemours' entry point ≈€5,173/month, Hanab's entry point €4,343/month) would only qualify a non-EU applicant under the **under-30** HSM threshold (€4,357/month) — meaning an older non-EU applicant hired at the bottom of either published band could actually fail to qualify for HSM sponsorship without a higher offer, and employers/applicants should check the exact band offered against age-specific thresholds before assuming sponsorship eligibility.
- The Tasman offshore technician entry salary (from €3,250/month gross) sits **below even the under-30 HSM threshold (€4,357/month)** and well below the 30-and-over threshold (€5,942/month) — this strongly implies that offshore wind technician entry-level roles, as advertised, are not viable as standalone Highly Skilled Migrant sponsorship vehicles for non-EU/non-EEA nationals; such candidates would likely need an EU/EEA passport, existing NL residency/work permit, or a different immigration route (e.g., a technician-specific "shortage occupation" work permit, if one exists) to take this specific role. This is an inference, not confirmed by a primary source in this pass.
- Engineer-tier roles at multinationals (Chemours-type) explicitly de-emphasizing Dutch fluency, combined with English-only postings (Hanab), suggests that for someone "newly established" in the Netherlands without Dutch fluency, the engineering/technical-specialist track is more immediately accessible than roles at Dutch-only-language employers (like Vattenfall's direct posting, which mandates Dutch).

### Gaps
- Foreign-degree/qualification-recognition requirements (e.g., whether a non-EU chemical engineering MSc needs formal Nuffic/IDW diploma evaluation before a Dutch employer or IND will accept it for an HSM application) were not directly researched in this pass and are not confirmed by any source above — this is a meaningful gap given the task's explicit interest in "foreign-degree/qualification recognition."
- IRATA/SPRAT rope-access certification requirements specific to the Dutch market (whether Dutch employers require IRATA Level 1/2/3 specifically, or accept other national rope-access schemes) were not found — general GWO wind-technician requirements were confirmed, but the combined rope-access + NDT inspector certification stack (which the task specifically asked about) remains unconfirmed for the NL market specifically.
- Whether a specific "shortage occupation" fast-track work-permit list exists in the Netherlands for offshore wind technicians (analogous to some other EU states' shortage-occupation visa routes) was not confirmed by any primary source in this research pass.
