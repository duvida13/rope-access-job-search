# Netherlands Tech/IT Sector — High-Paying Roles (2026)

## Which specific job titles pay the highest annual gross salary in tech/IT in the Netherlands, broken down by seniority?

### Takeaway
Engineering Manager, Cloud/Solution Architect, and Cybersecurity Specialist roles top the tech pay scale in the Netherlands (national averages €87k–€118k), followed closely by Data Scientist and Site Reliability Engineer; Software Engineer and DevOps Engineer sit in the upper-middle band, with Amsterdam consistently paying ~€15k–20k above the national average at every level. Most figures below are 2026 aggregator estimates (Glassdoor/SalaryExpert/ERI/Payscale/Levels.fyi), not agency-published bands, so treat exact numbers as indicative rather than authoritative.

### Cited Findings
- Software Engineer (NL average, 2026): overall ~€83,580/yr gross; entry-level (1–3 yrs) ~€59,253; senior (8+ yrs) ~€95,665; Amsterdam senior median ~€107,098 — [Levels.fyi / ERI / Glassdoor aggregate via WebSearch](https://www.levels.fyi/t/software-engineer/locations/netherlands)
- DevOps Engineer (NL, 2026): average ~€69,000–€76,863/yr; 25th–75th percentile €58,000–€82,000; senior average ~€83,000 (range €72,375–€101,798); Amsterdam average ~€89,664 — [Glassdoor Netherlands DevOps salary](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/netherlands-devops-engineer-salary-SRCH_IL.0,11_IN178_KO12,27.htm)
- Site Reliability Engineer (SRE) (NL, 2026): average ~€80,473/yr, base range €62k–€100k — [Payscale via WebSearch](https://www.payscale.com/research/NL/Job=Site_Reliability_Engineer_(SRE)/Salary/6380eb35/Experienced-Development-Operations-DevOps)
- Cloud Architect (NL, 2026): average ~€87,000–€109,405/yr; 25th–75th percentile €70,500–€109,400; Amsterdam average ~€91,000 (range €68,050–€100,750). Related "Cloud Solution Architect" title averages ~€95,000 (range €85,500–€113,750) — [Glassdoor / Jobicy / ERI via WebSearch](https://www.glassdoor.com/Salaries/netherlands-cloud-architect-salary-SRCH_IL.0,11_IN178_KO12,27.htm)
- Data Engineer: not separately quantified in this pass (see Gaps), but demand is explicitly called out as one of the top-4 most-requested ICT profiles by UWV — [UWV via WebSearch](https://www.uwv.nl/nl/arbeidsmarktinformatie/sector/ict)
- Data Scientist (NL, 2026): estimates range widely — one source ~€53,000–€174,000/yr span; another puts the average at ~€101,511/yr (range €70,144–€123,843) — [Shecodes/Payscale aggregate via WebSearch](https://www.payscale.com/research/NL/Job=Data_Scientist/Salary/05582c7c/Amsterdam)
- Cybersecurity Specialist (NL, 2026): national average ~€90,064/yr (~€43/hr); Amsterdam ~€106,079/yr; Rotterdam ~€100,918/yr; entry-level (1–3 yrs) ~€63,367; senior (8+ yrs) ~€101,381; 5-year projected salary growth +20.7% — [SalaryExpert/ERI via WebSearch](https://www.salaryexpert.com/salary/job/cyber-security-specialist/netherlands)
- Cybersecurity consultant/interim rates (Michael Page, agency guide): Project Managers €100–€120/hour; IT Managers/Digital Transformation Leaders €100–€135/hour; Cybersecurity consultants €90–€120/hour (contractor/interim rates, not salaried) — [Michael Page Netherlands](https://www.michaelpage.nl/en/advice/management-advice/staff-development/ITIM23-top-5-tech-interim-roles-and-salaries-in-the-netherlands)
- Product Manager (NL, 2026): average ~€81,053/yr + ~€3,639 bonus; entry-level (1–3 yrs) ~€57,687; senior (8+ yrs) ~€91,145 — [SalaryExpert via WebSearch](https://www.salaryexpert.com/salary/job/product-manager/netherlands)
- Engineering Manager (NL, 2026): average ~€117,506/yr + ~€8,789 bonus — the highest of all roles researched; entry-level (1–3 yrs) ~€81,122; senior (8+ yrs) ~€133,315 — [SalaryExpert via WebSearch](https://www.salaryexpert.com/salary/job/engineering-manager/netherlands)
- Real posted salary band, Lead/Senior Cyber Security Engineer, Stedin (Rotterdam, grid operator, posted Feb 24 2026): gross monthly salary €6,078.62–€10,413.04 at 40 hrs/week (≈€72,943–€124,957/yr before the 8% holiday allowance and a 14–24% personal budget on top), for 7+ years' experience — [Indeed.nl job posting, JOBSEARCH_100179](https://to.indeed.com/aa2xkkprzzj6)
- Netherlands remains one of Europe's top tech hubs; AI-specialist and cybersecurity-expert demand has pushed wages up ~8% year-over-year — [Michael Page Netherlands](https://www.michaelpage.nl/en/advice/management-advice/staff-development/ITIM23-top-5-tech-interim-roles-and-salaries-in-the-netherlands)

### Inferences
- Engineering Manager and Architect-level titles (Cloud/Solution/Security Architect) pay more than individual-contributor Software Engineer or DevOps roles at equivalent seniority, suggesting the highest-paying path for a newcomer is to target senior IC → architect/lead tracks rather than staying purely hands-on.
- The Stedin real posting (€6,078–€10,413/month, i.e., up to ~€125k/yr with allowances) sits well above the aggregator "average" cybersecurity salary of ~€90k, indicating that aggregator averages likely understate what senior/lead security engineering roles at Dutch utilities/critical-infrastructure employers actually pay.
- Aggregator salary figures (Glassdoor/SalaryExpert/ERI/Payscale/Levels.fyi) vary by 20–50% for the same role and location (e.g., Data Scientist €53k–€174k span), so any single figure should be treated as a rough midpoint, not precise.

### Gaps
- No agency-published (Michael Page/Robert Half/Page Personnel) 2026 salary-guide PDF could be opened directly during this session — search results confirmed the guides exist for 2026 but full tables were not extracted; only hourly interim/contractor rates for a few roles were retrievable. A follow-up fetch of https://www.michaelpage.nl/en/news-insights/studies/salary-benchmark or https://www.roberthalf.com/nl/en/insights/salary-guide would give more authoritative salaried (not hourly-contractor) figures.
- Data Engineer salary was not directly quantified with a number in this pass (only demand data was found); a dedicated search would be needed to fill this in.
- Junior/starter-level figures are missing for Cloud Architect, SRE, and Data Scientist — most sources only broke down "entry" vs "senior" for Software Engineer, Cybersecurity Specialist, Product Manager, and Engineering Manager.

## What is current demand/vacancy volume like for each role?

### Takeaway
The Dutch ICT labor market remains structurally tight in 2026 despite a slight cooling from the 2022–2023 peak; UWV explicitly names Cloud Engineers, Cybersecurity Specialists, Data Engineers, and IT-management roles (CIO/IT-Director/CISO) as the most in-demand ICT profiles nationally.

### Cited Findings
- Roughly 70,000–80,000 IT vacancies are open in the Netherlands on a fairly permanent/structural basis — [UWV / ICT-Recruiter aggregate via WebSearch](https://www.uwv.nl/nl/arbeidsmarktinformatie/sector/ict)
- Q4 2025: almost 18,800 ICT vacancies were open — [UWV via WebSearch](https://www.uwv.nl/nl/arbeidsmarktinformatie/sector/ict/arbeidskrapte-icters-neemt-af-maar-altijd-groot)
- Q1 2026: the labor market for ICT occupations is "krap tot zeer krap" (tight to very tight) in 34 of the Netherlands' 35 labor-market regions — [UWV/Dutch IT Channel via WebSearch](https://www.dutchitchannel.nl/news/639890/ict-arbeidsmarkt-blijft-ondanks-verlichting-krap)
- April 2026: 27.8% of ICT-sector companies report they cannot fully deliver services due to staff shortages — [UWV via WebSearch](https://www.uwv.nl/nl/arbeidsmarktinformatie/sector/ict/oplossingen-tekort-ict)
- Most-requested ICT profiles per UWV: Cloud Engineers, Cybersecurity Specialists, Data Engineers, and IT-management functions such as CIO, IT-Director, and CISO — [UWV via WebSearch](https://www.uwv.nl/nl/arbeidsmarktinformatie/sector/ict)
- Since 2023 there has been a declining trend in the raw number of ICT vacancies, but UWV states the structural scarcity (schaarste) persists — [UWV via WebSearch](https://www.uwv.nl/nl/arbeidsmarktinformatie/sector/ict)
- Demand for AI specialists and cybersecurity experts specifically has driven wage growth of ~8% year-over-year — [Michael Page Netherlands](https://www.michaelpage.nl/en/advice/management-advice/staff-development/ITIM23-top-5-tech-interim-roles-and-salaries-in-the-netherlands)
- Indicative posting volume on Indeed.nl (single-query snapshots, Sept 2026, not exhaustive): "data engineer" and "cybersecurity engineer" searches each returned a steady mix of listings from major employers (Just Eat Takeaway, TNO, Capgemini, Decathlon; Stedin, SPIE, Yunex Traffic, ASML) — [Indeed.nl searches, this session]

### Inferences
- Demand ranking (high → medium) based on the UWV named list plus posting patterns observed: **High** — Cloud Engineer/Architect, Cybersecurity Specialist/Engineer, Data Engineer, IT-management (CIO/CISO/IT-Director); **Medium** — Software/DevOps Engineer, Data Scientist, Product Manager, Engineering Manager (steady postings seen but not named explicitly by UWV as top-4).
- The combination of a declining raw vacancy count but persistent "very tight" regional labor-market ratings suggests demand is shifting toward more senior/specialized profiles (architects, leads, CISOs) rather than junior generalist roles — consistent with employers in the postings collected (ASML, Stedin) seeking 7–10+ years of experience for their most senior security roles.

### Gaps
- No hard per-role vacancy counts (e.g., "X thousand cloud architect postings this quarter") were found; UWV publishes shortage/tightness ratings by broad occupation category rather than granular open-vacancy counts per specific title.
- Indeed.nl search snapshots in this session are a small, single-day sample (10 results per query) and should not be read as a rigorous demand measure — they are included only as illustrative evidence of active hiring, not volume.

### Takeaway
Three concrete, currently-listed Indeed.nl postings were pulled directly via the Indeed job-search tool; only one (Stedin) disclosed a salary range — consistent with the general pattern that most Dutch tech job ads (especially at multinational/scale-up employers) omit salary, while public-sector/utility employers under collective labor agreements (CAO) do publish exact bands.

- For a newcomer prioritizing high pay with low language barrier, ASML- and multinational-tech-scale-up-style employers (English-only postings) are the more accessible route into the highest-paying niches (security architecture, senior data engineering); Dutch-fluency-gated employers like grid operators, though transparent on pay, are less accessible short-term.

## What are the entry barriers for each role (certifications/diplomas, Dutch language, work-permit/HSM sponsorship, foreign-degree recognition)?

### Takeaway
Most private-sector and multinational Dutch tech employers post exclusively in English and are highly accustomed to sponsoring the "highly skilled migrant" (HSM) visa; the main formal barrier is meeting the 2026 HSM gross monthly salary threshold, while Dutch-language fluency is a hard requirement mainly at Dutch-only/public-sector/critical-infrastructure employers rather than the tech sector broadly. Foreign-degree recognition (Nuffic) is helpful documentation but not a strict legal requirement for most IT roles since IT is an unregulated profession in the Netherlands.

### Cited Findings
- 2026 Highly Skilled Migrant (HSM) minimum gross monthly salary thresholds, effective January 1, 2026: **€5,942/month** for migrants aged 30 and older; **€4,357/month** for migrants under 30; a reduced threshold of **€3,122/month** applies to certain categories (e.g., recent graduates/orientation-year permit holders) — increases of ~4.5% versus 2025 — [Jobbatical / Baker Tilly / Grant Thornton / Executive Mobility Group via WebSearch](https://www.jobbatical.com/blog/netherlands-highly-skilled-migrant-salary-thresholds-2026)
- The HSM salary must be market-based, paid monthly into the migrant's own bank account, consist of the fixed gross monthly salary excluding holiday allowance, and exclude benefits-in-kind/variable pay from the calculation — [Baker Tilly via WebSearch](https://www.bakertilly.nl/en/inzichten/kennisartikel/new-threshold-amounts-for-expats-and-highly-skilled-migrants-in-2026)
- These 2026 thresholds apply to all HSM applications (new hires and extensions) submitted in 2026; a change-of-employer notification submitted in 2025 with a 2026 start date must still meet the 2026 threshold — [Baker Tilly via WebSearch](https://www.bakertilly.nl/en/inzichten/kennisartikel/new-threshold-amounts-for-expats-and-highly-skilled-migrants-in-2026)
- Nuffic "diplomawaardering" (diploma evaluation) compares a foreign diploma against the Dutch education system; for IT professionals specifically, either a university bachelor's degree (Nuffic-evaluated) OR five years of relevant work experience (three of which in IT) can satisfy the underlying HSM/employer qualification bar — [Nuffic/expat guidance via WebSearch](https://www.nuffic.nl/studeren-en-werken-in-nederland/werken-in-nederland-met-een-buitenlands-diploma)
- A Nuffic diploma evaluation is not legally binding — it does not itself grant a right to work or study; Dutch employers retain full discretion in hiring decisions regardless of the evaluation outcome — [Nuffic via WebSearch](https://www.nuffic.nl/studeren-en-werken-in-nederland/diplomawaardering-aanvragen)
- Because IT is generally not a legally regulated profession in the Netherlands, a Nuffic evaluation, while helpful, "may not be strictly required" for IT employment specifically — [Aggregated guidance via WebSearch](https://www.nuffic.nl/studeren-en-werken-in-nederland/werken-in-nederland-met-een-buitenlands-diploma)
- Real-posting evidence on language requirement: the ASML CS&S Security Architect and Just Eat Takeaway Data Engineer postings are written entirely in English with no Dutch-language requirement stated, while the Stedin Lead Cyber Security Engineer posting explicitly requires Dutch language at C1/C2 (near-native/fluent) level — [Indeed.nl postings, this session](https://to.indeed.com/aaj7pr926f9j)
- The ASML Security Architect posting states relevant certifications (CISSP, CCSP, ISSAP, SABSA, TOGAF, or equivalent) are "required," but explicitly notes that demonstrated practical experience, architectural judgment, and delivery of security outcomes are "valued more highly than certifications alone" — [Indeed.nl, JOBSEARCH_100131](https://to.indeed.com/aaj7pr926f9j)

### Inferences
- For a newcomer without Dutch fluency, the practical entry barrier for most of the highest-paying tech titles (software engineering, cloud/security architecture, data engineering at multinational employers) is primarily meeting the HSM salary threshold (€4,357–€5,942/month gross depending on age) plus employer sponsorship — not language or formal diploma recognition — since IT is unregulated and English-only postings are common at large/international employers.
- Certifications (CISSP, CCSP, TOGAF, SABSA for security/architecture roles) function as a credibility signal and sometimes a stated "requirement," but real postings suggest they are negotiable if practical experience is strong — this lowers the effective barrier for experienced candidates without the exact certification in hand.
- Employers most likely to require Dutch fluency (C1/C2) — grid operators, likely also government/semi-public bodies — are simultaneously the ones most likely to publish exact CAO salary scales, creating a trade-off between pay transparency and language accessibility for newcomers.

### Gaps
- No official Dutch-government or IND source was directly fetched confirming the general (non-HSM) tech-sector split between English-only and Dutch-required roles at scale; the language-requirement conclusion here rests on a 3-posting sample from Indeed.nl, not a systematic survey.
- Sector-specific certification requirements were only characterized in depth for cybersecurity/security-architecture (via the ASML posting); analogous certification norms for Cloud Architect (e.g., AWS/Azure/GCP certifications), Data Engineer, or Product Manager roles were not separately confirmed with a cited source in this pass.
- The "reduced" HSM threshold (€3,122/month) was found but its exact eligibility criteria (e.g., recent graduate / orientation-year permit holder status) were not fully detailed in the sources retrieved — a follow-up read of the IND's own highly-skilled-migrant page would clarify who exactly qualifies for this reduced rate.
