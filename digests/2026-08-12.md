Method: 5 parallel background research agents (UK/Ireland; Netherlands/Belgium/Germany/Switzerland; France/Spain/Portugal; Italy/Norway/Denmark; pan-European aggregators) using English + local-language terms (cordiste, Seilzugangstechniker/Hoehenarbeiter/Industriekletterer, tecnico de acesso por cordas/trabajos verticales, operatore su fune, tilkomsttekniker, touwtoegangstechnicus) via WebSearch. Dedup note: checked against state/seen_jobs.json by exact URL (412 previously-seen links). Aggregator/portal links (Indeed viewjob tokens, LinkedIn, Jooble, findajob.dwp.gov.uk, finn.no, etc.) can regenerate a fresh URL for what is functionally the same underlying vacancy reported on an earlier day — skim titles/companies you recognize from prior digests before re-applying.
