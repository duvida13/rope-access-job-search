# Rope Access Job Digest — 2026-08-17

**30 new postings/leads** across 10 countries.

Method: 5 parallel background research agents (UK/Ireland; Netherlands/Belgium/Germany/Switzerland; France/Spain/Portugal; Italy/Norway/Denmark; pan-European aggregators covering Austria/Poland/Sweden/Lithuania/Czechia) using English + local-language terms (cordiste, técnico de acceso mediante cuerdas/acesso por cordas, Seilzugangstechniker/Höhenarbeiter/Industriekletterer, touwtoegangstechnicus, operatore su fune, tilkomsttekniker) via WebSearch. Company career-site and job-board WebFetch was blocked by network egress for essentially every domain tried this run, so all entries are sourced from search-result snippets and aggregator listings rather than independently re-verified page fetches — links are real as surfaced by search, but live status was not independently re-confirmed by fetch for every entry.

Dedup note: checked candidates against state/seen_jobs.json by exact URL. Postings flagged as wind-turbine blade/service technician work are shown for visibility only per Luis's request, even though that is explicitly not his background.
