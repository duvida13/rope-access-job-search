# Rope Access Job Digest — 2026-08-18

**59 new postings/leads** across 13 countries.

Method: 5 parallel background research agents (UK/Ireland; Netherlands/Belgium/Germany/Switzerland; France/Spain/Portugal; Italy/Norway/Denmark; pan-European aggregators covering Austria/Poland/Sweden/Finland/Lithuania/Estonia) using English + local-language terms (cordiste, técnico de trabajos verticales/acesso por cordas, Seilzugangstechniker/Höhenarbeiter/Industriekletterer, tilkomsttekniker, operatore su fune) via WebSearch and the Indeed jobs API. Company career-site and job-board WebFetch was blocked by network egress for essentially every domain tried this run (confirmed 403s from the proxy), and the Indeed API tool was rate-limited partway through several searches, so most entries are sourced from search-result snippets and the Indeed API's own apply links rather than independently re-verified page fetches — links are real as surfaced by search/API, but live status was not independently re-confirmed by fetch for every entry.

Dedup note: checked all candidates against state/seen_jobs.json by exact URL (44 candidates were already-seen evergreen listings and were skipped). Postings flagged as wind-turbine blade/service technician work are shown for visibility only per Luis's request, even though that is explicitly not his background.
